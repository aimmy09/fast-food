How students can stretch the budget further

Small habits that quietly cut your monthly food spend.

  • Use bank-card discounts. Several chains run card offers of up to 40% off on selected banks — check which card gets you the best deal before you pay.
  • Split duo deals. Two-person combos almost always beat two single meals. Share with a friend and halve the cost.
  • Order value combos, not à la carte. A burger, side and drink bundled together costs far less than buying each item separately.
  • Order direct from the restaurant's app. Skipping the delivery platform often unlocks app-only prices — brands like Simply Sufi XPRS push their best offers this way.
  • Hunt the midnight and flash deals. Late-night and limited-time offers are where the real student savings hide.
  • Chase value burger deals. Local chains run some of the cheapest combos in the city — the XPRS burger deals from Simply Sufi XPRS are an easy budget win.

Use bank-card discounts (some chains offer up to 40% off on selected cards), split two-person duo deals, order value combos instead of individual items, order directly from a restaurant's own app for app-only offers, and use late-night or midnight deals.
